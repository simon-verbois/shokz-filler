Music, audiobooks and local MP3s share the same device, with dedicated screens to keep things clear.

Search songs and playlists, then use + to build your send list. It stays with you as you browse. Send to Shokz opens a review of the songs and destination before anything downloads.

Use my YouTube Music playlists

In Settings, click Connect next to YouTube Music. Open your browser, sign in and return to the app to import the session. Then open My playlists. Public search works without an account.

One book. One folder. Numbered chapters.

Connect your Audiobookshelf server in Settings. Select books and choose Send to Shokz: each chapter becomes a numbered MP3, copied in playback order. Available chapter metadata is used when valid; otherwise the source-file order is preserved.

Your files, in their place.

Choose your detected Shokz or use Browse… to select its mounted folder. Create folders, rename and move files. Add MP3 imports local music: compatible files copy directly, others are converted to the target format without changing your originals.

Deletion asks for confirmation. Name conflicts preserve both files. Recovery tools are in the overflow menu.

Know what is happening.

Follow downloads, conversion and copies in Transfers. Use Cancel selected for active or queued items, or Retry selected after a failure or cancellation. The sidebar indicator stays visible on every screen.

Interrupted copies remain distinguishable from complete MP3s. Reconnect the same device before retrying or resuming.

Hear which chapter comes next.

Optional Piper announcements run offline, with eight bundled voices across French, English, German, Dutch and Spanish. Set separate text for the first and later chapters. Default speed is 100%, followed by 1.5 seconds of silence.

Use $book_name, $author_name, $chapter_number and $chapter_title. Try Preview, then Save settings. Changes apply to books queued afterwards.

From your first click to your next swim.

For music, a send list keeps you in control of the tracks, playback order and destination.

  1. Connect your Shokz

    Connect the headphones by USB. In Shokz files, select the mounted device or use Browse…. Check the name and available space in the sidebar.

  2. Build your send list

    Search in YouTube Music or open My playlists. Use + beside a song or playlist. Your list keeps its order across searches; adding an item does not start a download.

  3. Review, then send

    Choose Send to Shokz. Reorder or remove songs, then click Next. Select an existing folder or create one, and click Send.

  4. Follow progress and eject

    Open Transfers to watch progress or retry a failed item. Wait until all operations finish, then eject the headphones through your operating system before unplugging them.

Before you unplug.

A few things to know about your files, your headset and your accounts.

Which headsets are compatible?

Shokz models with USB MP3 storage, including OpenSwim Pro. Unknown volume names can be selected manually. Numbered filenames and sequential copies help preserve playback order; behaviour depends on the model and firmware.

What happens if I send something twice?

Recognised identical tracks and exports are skipped. Unidentified files and filename conflicts are preserved. A book with different sources or announcement settings gets a separate export folder.

What needs Internet access?

YouTube Music and Audiobookshelf need access to their services. Local MP3 import and Piper announcements work offline. Account sessions are stored locally; Disconnect or Disconnect / forget removes the saved app credentials.

Can I transfer a long audiobook?

Books are split into numbered chapter MP3s, with a conservative 4 GiB limit per output file. Keep enough free space on your computer for the sources and conversion cache, and on the headphones for the result.
