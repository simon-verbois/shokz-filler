02 / For stories that go the distance

Keep the thread.
To the very last chapter.

One MP3 per book prevents the headset from reordering its separate files. The content follows the Audiobookshelf track order, even when filenames are not alphabetical.

Before the book, an optional local male voice can announce its title and author. Default speed: 95%. Then 3 seconds of silence, and the story begins.

Spoken introduction→3 s of silence→Complete book

Piper and its voices are bundled. Speech synthesis works offline.

03 / Getting started

A few clicks before
your first length.

The app buttons are in English. This guide uses their exact names so you can find them easily.

Step 1 / The starting point

Connect your Shokz.

  1. Connect the headset by USB and open its storage on your computer.
  2. In Shokz files, choose the detected device. If needed, use Select device folder to select its mounted volume.
  3. Check its name and available space. For music, create or choose a destination folder.

Wait for transfers to finish, then eject the device from your operating system before unplugging it.

Import MP3 files already on my computer

Open your chosen folder in the device tab, click Add local MP3 and select your files. The app copies them without re-encoding: their quality and metadata stay as they are. Use New folder to create a folder and Move selected… to organise files already on the headset.

Step 2 / For music

Find your next rhythm.

  1. In YouTube Music, enter a query and choose Songs or Playlists, then Search.
  2. Select tracks using Ctrl or Shift, then Send selection to Shokz. Choose a folder if prompted.
  3. For a playlist, double-click to open it and use Download whole playlist. Its folder is created automatically.
  4. Follow the steps in the queue. Something failed? Select its row and use Retry selected.
Find playlists from my account

Click Connect YouTube Music and follow the dialog to import headers from a /browse request in your music.youtube.com session. Then use My playlists. Public search works without signing in.

Step 2 / For audiobooks

Choose the stories to take along.

Audiobookshelf
  1. In Audiobookshelf, enter Server (the full URL), Username and Password, then click Connect.
  2. Choose your library, search for a title or author, and select books to transfer using Ctrl or Shift.
  3. Click Send selected books to Shokz. A dialog opens for each book: keep or disable the spoken introduction, then choose Queue book. Skip book leaves that book out.
  4. The app downloads, converts, merges and copies the book into its own folder. Wait for the complete status.

Your Audiobookshelf account needs download permission. The password is saved only if you tick the corresponding option.

Step 3 / A personal touch

An introduction in your own words.

  1. Open Settings and choose the male voice Tom for French or John for English.
  2. Keep the 95% speed or adjust it between 60 and 120%.
  3. Customise the text with $book_name and $author_name. The preview shows the result; $$ inserts a dollar sign.
  4. Click Save settings. These choices apply to books added to the queue afterwards.

Example text

You are listening to $book_name.
A book by $author_name.

One more thing?

Before you
unplug.

Which headsets are compatible?

The app targets Shokz headsets with USB MP3 storage, including OpenSwim Pro. If a volume’s name is not recognised, select its folder manually. Playback behaviour depends on the model and firmware.

Do chapters stay in the right order?

For Audiobookshelf exports, tracks are merged in the order supplied by the server. The headset therefore cannot reorder chapters within this single MP3. The app does not guarantee the playback order of separate tracks or books.

What if I transfer the same content twice?

Recognised identical exports are skipped. Unidentified files and name conflicts are preserved. A new version of a book, for example with a different introduction, gets a new numbered folder.

How do I resume after a disconnection?

Reconnect and select the same device. Use Retry selected for a queue item, or Resume interrupted copies for interrupted copies. The app validates partial files before resuming and checks integrity before finalising the file.

Does the voice need Internet access?

No. Piper and the voice models are included in current portable builds. The announcement is generated locally. Downloading media requires access to YouTube Music or your Audiobookshelf server.

Is there a limit for long books?

Exports that would exceed the conservative 4 GiB per-file MP3 limit are refused. Allow space on your computer for source and intermediate files too.
