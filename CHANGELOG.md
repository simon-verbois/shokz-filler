## 2026.44.1 - 2026-10-28

- The application is now called **Shokz Filler**. Your settings and cache folders are moved automatically, and MP3s already on your device are still recognised.
- Audiobooks with chapters now export one MP3 per chapter, even from a single m4b file or when the server's chapter times have gaps or drift.
- The sidebar no longer shows "Choose a device in Shokz files" when nothing is connected.
- YouTube Music shows the same "No Shokz selected" hint as Audiobookshelf when no device is connected.
- New Settings option to convert all audio to mono (off by default; saved with Save settings).
- **Save settings** is greyed out until something changed, and sits in the Settings footer.
- Transfers rows are single cards, like the library lists, instead of separately selectable cells.
- New **Clear finished** button in Transfers.
- The sidebar shows the app version and a Changelog button on every page.
- The sidebar device header no longer shows a headphones icon.
